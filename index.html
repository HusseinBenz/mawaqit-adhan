Windows tray app · v1.0.0-beta.1

The adhan, called on time, from your own masjid

Mawaqit Adhan sits quietly in your Windows tray and plays the call to prayer using a masjid's own published Mawaqit timetable — five daily prayers, time-zone and DST aware, no account and no server required.

What it does

Any Mawaqit masjid

Search by name or city, or paste a Mawaqit link. The full-year timetable is cached for offline use and refreshed automatically.

Five bundled adhans

Five recordings included, with a separate Fajr voice/volume, per-prayer switches, and support for additional or imported recordings.

Correct times, everywhere

Time-zone and daylight-saving handling, including half-hour DST transitions, mapped from the masjid's own IANA zone.

Iqama & Jumu'ah

Iqama offsets or fixed times, Hijri adjustment, and a separate Friday Jumu'ah display alongside the regular Dhuhr call.

Plays well with other audio

Fades other apps' volume down for the adhan and restores it afterward, with a media play/pause signal in between.

No installer, no telemetry

Portable executable, no admin rights, no third-party runtime beyond .NET Framework 4.8 (already on Windows 10/11), and nothing phoned home.

Requirements

  • Windows 7 SP1, 8.1, 10 (1607+) or 11, with .NET Framework 4.8 or later.
  • The app must be running and the PC awake to call prayers on time.
  • Internet access to look up a masjid and refresh its timetable; a cached calendar keeps working offline afterward.
